# Ishikaa Farms --- Brand & Design System

**Version:** 1.0\
**Brand:** Ishikaa Farms\
**Primary category:** Fresh, naturally cultivated farm produce\
**Brand character:** Premium • Natural • Warm • Trustworthy • Modern •
Carefully cultivated

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand Direction

Ishikaa Farms should look like a **premium farm brand**, not a generic
agricultural supplier.

The visual language should communicate:

-   Freshness without looking clinical
-   Natural cultivation without looking rustic or homemade
-   Premium quality without looking luxurious or expensive
-   Warmth without becoming playful or childish
-   Trust and care without excessive claims
-   A contemporary Indian farm identity with subtle botanical/earthed
    character

The target reaction is:

> "This feels like a serious, premium local food brand."

The design should feel considered even when used on something as small
as a WhatsApp image, sticker, product label, visiting card, Instagram
post, invoice, or packaging.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Visual Identity

## Primary aesthetic

**Modern botanical luxury + natural farm minimalism**

Think:

-   Soft cream paper
-   Deep forest/olive green
-   Muted earthy gold
-   Warm mushroom/beige tones
-   Fine botanical line drawings
-   Elegant serif typography
-   Generous whitespace
-   Subtle paper texture
-   Rounded or gently organic shapes
-   High-quality food photography

Avoid:

-   Bright neon greens
-   Generic "organic farm" clip-art
-   Cartoon mushrooms
-   Heavy gradients
-   Excessive leaves everywhere
-   Plastic-looking 3D effects
-   Loud agricultural imagery
-   Cheap-looking badges
-   Too many fonts
-   Overcrowded layouts

For most designs:

**60% Cream / light neutral**\

# 5. Typography

The typography is one of the most important parts of the Ishikaa Farms
identity.

## Primary Display Typeface

### Cormorant Garamond

Use for: - "Ishikaa" - Major headlines - Premium campaign statements -
Hero typography

Recommended weights:

-   Regular
-   Medium
-   SemiBold

The word **Ishikaa** should generally use a refined high-contrast serif
treatment with generous spacing and breathing room.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Secondary Typeface

### Montserrat

Use for: - FARMS - Product information - Contact details - Buttons -
Labels - Supporting copy - Numbers - Packaging information

Recommended weights:

-   Regular
-   Medium
-   SemiBold

Use uppercase with increased letter spacing for premium small labels.

Example:

`F A R M S`

or:

`FRESH • PURE • SUSTAINABLE`

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Optional Script Accent

Use a restrained handwritten/script font only for occasional emotional
phrases.

Good direction:

-   Cormorant Infant Italic
-   Allura
-   Parisienne

Use script for **one short phrase only**, such as:

> Let's grow something healthy together.

Never use script for: - Contact details - Product names - Prices -
Important information - Long paragraphs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Logo System

The logo should be treated as a complete identity system rather than a
single image.

## Primary logo

Circular botanical emblem + mushroom illustration +:

**Ishikaa**\
**FARMS**

The emblem should combine:

-   Oyster mushroom forms
-   Fine botanical foliage
-   A subtle natural growth element
-   Circular/open-circle geometry

The illustration should feel **botanical and premium**, not
cartoon-like.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Logo proportions

Preferred hierarchy:

1.  Symbol
2.  Ishikaa
3.  FARMS
4.  Optional descriptor/tagline

"Ishikaa" must remain the dominant word.

"FARMS" should be noticeably smaller.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Logo clear space

Maintain minimum clear space around the logo equal to approximately the
height of the lowercase **x** in "Ishikaa".

Do not place text, photographs, borders, or icons inside this clear
zone.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### B. Dark Background Logo

Cream + muted gold on forest green.

### C. One-Color Logo

Forest green only.

### D. Symbol Only

Used for: - Social profile image - Sticker - Packaging seal - Favicons -

### E. Horizontal Logo

Used when vertical space is limited.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Logo Don'ts

Never:

-   Stretch the logo
-   Squash the logo
-   Rotate it
-   Add drop shadows
-   Add glow effects
-   Use bright green
-   Add random gradients
-   Put it over a busy photograph
-   Change the typeface
-   Rearrange the logo elements
-   Add unnecessary text around it

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Illustration Style

Illustrations should use:

**Fine botanical engraving / vintage botanical drawing influence**

Characteristics:

-   Thin controlled lines
-   Natural proportions
-   Minimal shading
-   Warm beige/brown mushroom tones
-   Deep green botanical details
-   Slightly imperfect organic forms

Avoid:

-   Cartoon outlines
-   Emoji-style mushrooms
-   Flat clip-art
-   Highly saturated illustrations
-   Hyper-realistic scientific diagrams

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Photography Style

Photography should feel:

**Fresh • Natural • Premium • Honest**

Preferred imagery:

-   Fresh oyster mushrooms
-   Close-up mushroom clusters
-   Natural growing environment
-   Harvest moments
-   Clean baskets/trays
-   Hands handling produce
-   Soft daylight
-   Neutral backgrounds
-   Natural textures

Lighting:

-   Soft daylight
-   Warm-neutral white balance
-   Gentle shadows
-   Low-to-medium contrast

Avoid:

-   Harsh flash
-   Oversaturated greens
-   Artificial studio lighting
-   Busy backgrounds
-   Excessive filters
-   Stock-photo-looking farms

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Image Composition

Preferred composition:

### 70/30 rule

Approximately 70% visual subject and 30% breathing room.

Use negative space intentionally.

For social media:

-   Subject can occupy one side
-   Text occupies the opposite side
-   Keep the logo away from the photograph's busiest region

For packaging:

-   Product should be visually dominant
-   Brand should remain immediately recognizable
-   Information should be secondary

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Graphic Elements

Use these recurring elements:

### Botanical branch

Thin olive/forest-green line art.

### Circular frame

Use incomplete/open circles rather than heavy closed rings.

### Fine divider lines

Muted gold or beige.

### Small dots

# 22. Tone of Voice

The visual identity should be premium, but the written voice should
remain human.

Use words such as:

-   Fresh
-   Carefully cultivated
-   Naturally grown
-   Locally grown
-   Harvested fresh
-   Quality
-   Care
-   Nourishing
-   Thoughtfully cultivated

Avoid excessive marketing language such as:

-   Ultimate
-   #1
-   Revolutionary
-   Miracle
-   Guaranteed
-   Best in India
-   100% chemical-free

Only make factual claims that can actually be supported.

# 24. Layout Philosophy

The most important rule:

> **Luxury comes from restraint.**

Every design should have:

-   One dominant message
-   One dominant image
-   One dominant brand mark
-   One supporting accent

If something can be removed without losing meaning, remove it.

Whitespace is a brand element.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 25. Shape Language

Preferred:

-   Circles
-   Arcs
-   Organic curves
-   Rounded rectangles
-   Soft botanical silhouettes

Avoid:

-   Sharp aggressive geometry
-   Excessive boxes
-   Heavy borders
-   Random decorative shapes

The overall silhouette should feel organic and calm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 26. Material & Texture

Preferred textures:

-   Fine paper grain
-   Soft natural fiber
-   Subtle beige texture
-   Matte surfaces
-   Natural wood
-   Soil/moss texture in photography

Texture should be barely noticeable.

Never use obvious fake paper overlays.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 27. Premium Rules

To make the design look expensive:

1.  Use fewer elements.
2.  Increase whitespace.
3.  Use better photography.
4.  Use consistent typography.
5.  Keep the color palette narrow.
6.  Use gold only as an accent.
7.  Keep icons consistent.
8.  Align everything precisely.
9.  Avoid excessive badges.
10. Make every element earn its place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 31. The Ishikaa Farms Rule

Whenever creating something new, it should look like it belongs to **the
same family** as the logo and visiting card even if the asset is seen
completely on its own.

The combination to preserve is:

**Elegant serif + forest green + warm cream + restrained gold +
botanical line art + real fresh produce + generous whitespace + precise
layout.**

That combination is the recognizable Ishikaa Farms visual signature.
